What this website is

This website presents VeloPulse, an indoor cycling training studio together with the performance analytics of Intervals.icu. The site itself is only informative: it sells nothing, asks for no registration and collects none of your data (see the privacy policy).

The VeloPulse app

VeloPulse runs in the browser (on Chrome, Edge or another Chromium-based browser, for the Bluetooth sensors) and as an app installed on Android, Windows, macOS and Linux; for iPhone you need to build the app with Xcode. The app has no account system of its own: to see your training data you use the API key of your Intervals.icu account, or demo mode with sample data. You are responsible for your API key and for keeping it private. Use of the app is governed by its own terms of service, which you find inside it, on the "termini.html" page: this page does not replace them and does not contradict them.

The app connects to third-party Bluetooth sensors (trainer, power, cadence, heart rate strap) and to external services (Intervals.icu, and the services where you upload the .fit files, such as Strava and Garmin Connect). How these services work does not depend on VeloPulse: an outage or a change in an external service may limit some features of the app.

Downloading the app from this website

The download buttons point to the VeloPulse release pages on GitHub, not to a file hosted on this site. GitHub is not a service of VeloPulse: we do not guarantee that it is always available. Before you install a file downloaded from the internet, check that your system allows it (for example the permission to install from an external source on Android).

Limits of liability

The metrics that VeloPulse calculates (normalized power, intensity factor, critical power, W' reserve, form, fatigue, recovery and the like) are estimates, for information and training purposes. They are not a medical assessment and they do not replace the advice of a doctor or of a qualified coach, in particular if you have health conditions that need care during physical activity.

Anyone who trains with VeloPulse does so at their own risk. The app shows the data as it receives it from Intervals.icu or from the Bluetooth sensors you connect: we do not check and do not guarantee that it is accurate.
