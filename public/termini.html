What VeloPulse offers

VeloPulse is a demo version of a dashboard for indoor cycling training: it shows activities, wellness, calendar, and power curve based on the data from your Intervals.icu account, and offers a training studio with support for Bluetooth sensors. Being a demo, we do not guarantee continuity of service, uninterrupted availability, or freedom from errors or interruptions.

You need an Intervals.icu API key

To use the app with your real data you must have an Intervals.icu account and enter your personal API key. By using VeloPulse with your key you also accept the terms of use and privacy policy of Intervals.icu, which remains a service independent of VeloPulse. Alternatively, you can use demo mode with sample data, with no key required.

Rules of use

  • Use only your own API key, or one from an account you are authorized to use.
  • Do not use the app for unlawful purposes or to violate Intervals.icu's terms.
  • Do not share your API key with other people: anyone who knows it can read your training data on Intervals.icu.
