What VeloPulse is

VeloPulse is a demo dashboard for indoor cycling training. It is an application that runs entirely in your browser: it has no account system of its own and no server that receives or stores your data. This page explains what data the app processes and how.

Your Intervals.icu API key

To see your real data, you paste your personal Intervals.icu API key into the login screen. The app:

  • stores the key in plain text in the browser's local storage (localStorage) on the device you are using;
  • uses it only to authenticate you directly with Intervals.icu when the browser calls its APIs.

The key is never sent to a VeloPulse server, because VeloPulse does not have one. Anyone with access to your device or browser can read the stored key: do not enter it on a shared or public computer, and sign out of the app when you are done.

Demo mode

You can also use VeloPulse in demo mode, with made-up sample data: in this case you do not need to enter any key, and no real data is read.

On the published demo of this site, demo mode is the only mode available. The site does not ask you for any API key, has no login screen, and does not read any real data from Intervals.icu. On opening, it also clears any key and data that a previous version of the site may have stored in your browser's local storage. To connect your real Intervals.icu profile, you need the Android app, the desktop program, or the site built from source.

Data downloaded from Intervals.icu and settings

Once you sign in with your key, the app downloads activities, wellness data, calendar events, and the power curve from Intervals.icu, and stores them in the browser's local storage so they load faster next time. Your settings too (FTP, heart rate zones, weight, unit preferences) stay there only.

How long data is kept, and how to delete it

Your API key, downloaded data, and settings stay in the browser's local storage until you delete them. To delete them:

  • press "Disconnect account" in the app, or
  • clear this site's browsing data in your browser.

Since VeloPulse has no server, there is no account to delete with us: your training data stays on Intervals.icu under their rules, not ours.

Third-party services

Intervals.icu — is the independent data controller for your training data. Your browser talks directly to its servers when it downloads or sends data: VeloPulse is not an intermediary. See Intervals.icu's privacy policy.

OpenStreetMap and CARTO — when you open a route map, your browser downloads the map tiles from these services: they receive the IP address of whoever is viewing the map at that moment.

Bluetooth sensors — if you connect a smart trainer, a power meter, a cadence sensor, or a heart rate strap, the app communicates with those devices directly from the browser. The data stays on the device you are using and is not sent to any VeloPulse server.

.fit files

The .fit files of recorded sessions are generated by the device and downloaded directly by you: they never pass through any VeloPulse server.

What VeloPulse does not do

The app itself contains no analytics code, no profiling cookies, no emails, no payments and no push notifications. The website that hosts the app counts visits, as described below.

Where this site is hosted

This demo site is hosted on institorAI's infrastructure. The demo version is private: it only opens with an access link, is not indexed, and is meant for testing and demonstrations.

Visit statistics on this website

This website uses the visit statistics of institorAI. They set no cookies and write nothing on your device. This section applies to the website only, not to the desktop app or the Android app.

Each visit records:

  • the page you visit, without the query string;
  • the site you came from;
  • the campaign of the link, if it has one (utm_source, utm_medium, utm_campaign);
  • your country;
  • the kind of device, and the family of your browser and operating system.

Your IP address and your user agent only serve to compute a fingerprint that changes every day, which tells a repeated visit from a new one. Neither of them is stored. Raw data is kept for 30 days and daily totals for 13 months. The legal basis is legitimate interest: counting visits to a website. The data controller is the owner of this site; institorAI processes the data on their behalf.
