Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 27, 2026

Who we are

VeloLab (“the app”, “we”) is an indoor cycling and training analytics app, available as a web app, an Android/iOS app and a desktop program.

Data controller: Francesco Ranellucci, Italy. Contact for any privacy request: fra.ranellucci@gmail.com. We answer within 30 days.

Summary

  • We only use your data to show you your own training. Nobody else sees it.
  • We do not sell, rent or share your data, and we do not use it for advertising.
  • We do not use your data to train, fine-tune or feed any AI or machine-learning model.
  • You can disconnect any service and delete your data at any time.

Ways to use the app

Demo mode — made-up sample data, no account, no real data read. On the published demo site this is the only mode available.

Local mode — you paste your Intervals.icu API key. The key and the data downloaded from Intervals.icu stay in your browser’s local storage on your device; your browser talks to Intervals.icu directly.

Account mode — you create an account (e-mail and password, or Google sign-in). Your data is stored in our database so it is available on all your devices. The rest of this page mainly describes this mode.

What data we process, and why

  • Account data: e-mail address, password (stored only as a hash by our authentication provider), and, with Google sign-in, your name and profile picture. Purpose: letting you sign in. Legal basis: contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).
  • Profile and settings: FTP, weight, heart-rate and power zones, units, city/country if you enter them. Purpose: computing your training metrics. Legal basis: contract.
  • Training data: activities (date, duration, distance, power, heart rate, cadence, speed, elevation, GPS route), wellness data (e.g. HRV, resting heart rate, sleep), calendar events and power curve, from the sources you connect or the files you import. Weight, heart rate and wellness data are health-related data: we process them only because you explicitly ask us to, by connecting a source or importing a file (Art. 9(2)(a) GDPR). You can withdraw this consent at any time by disconnecting the source or deleting the data.
  • Sessions recorded in the app: power, cadence, heart rate and speed read from your Bluetooth sensors, and the generated .fit file. Legal basis: contract.
  • Technical data: the hosting provider processes your IP address and request details to deliver the app and protect it from abuse; error logs are kept for a short time to fix problems. Legal basis: legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

Connected services

Strava

If you press “Connect Strava”, you are sent to Strava, which asks you to allow VeloLab to read your activities, including private ones (scopes read and activity:read_all). We do not ask for permission to read your Strava profile or to post on your behalf.

  • What we collect: for each activity, its Strava id, name, sport type, start time, distance, moving and elapsed time, elevation gain, average and maximum speed, heart rate and power, energy, cadence, whether it was on a trainer, and the summary GPS route with start and end coordinates. We also store your Strava athlete id and the access tokens Strava gives us.
  • How: when you press “Sync Strava” (the last 60 days), and automatically when Strava notifies us that you created, changed or deleted an activity.
  • Who sees it: only you. Strava data is never shown to other users, never combined into public pages, rankings or feeds, and never given to third parties, advertisers or AI providers.
  • Tokens: Strava access tokens are kept on our servers only, never sent to your browser, and used only to read your own activities.
  • Deletions on Strava: if you delete an activity on Strava, we delete our copy within 48 hours.
  • Withdrawing consent and deleting Strava data: press the disconnect button next to Strava in Profile → Data Sources, or revoke VeloLab in your Strava settings (Settings → My Apps). In both cases we revoke our access and permanently delete all activities imported from Strava and your Strava tokens. When you disconnect from the app you see a written confirmation with the number of deleted activities and the time of deletion; if you revoke from Strava, we delete everything within 30 days at the latest (normally within minutes), and you can ask us by e-mail for a written confirmation.
  • Strava may collect and use data about how you use the Strava API through our app (usage data) according to Strava’s privacy policy. VeloLab is an independent app and is not affiliated with, sponsored or endorsed by Strava.

Intervals.icu

If you connect Intervals.icu you enter your personal API key. In account mode the key is stored in our database, readable only by your account, and used to download your activities, wellness data, calendar and power curve, and to upload sessions you record, when you ask us to or when you turn on automatic upload. In local mode it stays in your browser. You can remove it at any time with the disconnect button. See Intervals.icu’s privacy policy.

Garmin .fit files

If you import a .fit file, it is read on your device and the resulting activity is stored in your account. The file itself is not uploaded.

Where your data is stored, and who processes it

We use these providers (sub-processors), bound by data processing agreements:

Supabase — authentication and database (account, profile, connections, training data). Data is stored in the European Union (Ireland).

Cloudflare — hosting of the web app and of the server functions that talk to Strava; processes IP addresses and request logs.

Google — only if you choose “Sign in with Google”.

OpenStreetMap and CARTO — map tiles. When you open a map, your browser downloads tiles from them, so they receive your IP address.

Some of these providers are based in or may process data in the United States. In that case transfers rely on the EU–US Data Privacy Framework or on the European Commission’s Standard Contractual Clauses.

Bluetooth sensors are read directly by your device. Live sensor data is not sent anywhere until you save the session.

Data on your device

The app keeps settings, a cache of your data for faster loading and offline use, and sessions recorded on that device in the browser’s local storage. You can delete it by signing out and clearing this site’s data in your browser (or uninstalling the app). We use no advertising or tracking cookies; sign-in uses only the storage strictly needed to keep you logged in.

How long we keep data

  • Account, profile and training data: as long as your account exists.
  • Data from a connected service: until you disconnect that service (for Strava, see above) or delete your account.
  • Technical logs: a few days at the hosting provider.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you can ask us at any time to access your data and receive a copy of it, correct it, delete it, restrict or object to its processing, and receive it in a portable format. You can withdraw your consent at any time, without affecting what was done before. To delete your whole account and all its data, or for any other request, write to the contact address above; we confirm the deletion in writing. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority, in Italy the Garante per la protezione dei dati personali.

Security

Data travels over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Database access is restricted so that each account can only read its own rows, and third-party tokens are handled only on our servers. If we ever discover a breach affecting your data, we will notify you and the competent authorities as the law requires.

Children

The app is not intended for people under 16.

Hosted demo

The private demo site is hosted on institorAI’s infrastructure, works only in demo mode and counts visits without cookies: the page, referrer, campaign parameters, country, device type and browser family. IP address and user agent only compute a fingerprint that changes every day and are not stored. Raw data is kept for 30 days, daily totals for 13 months.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy in a way that affects how your data is used, we will tell you in the app and, where required, ask for your consent again.
